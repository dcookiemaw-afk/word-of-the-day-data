Privacy Policy for Word of the Day Effective Date: September 28, 2026 Word of the Day ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (Word of the Day, package com.mawtastic.wordoftheday) handles your information. 1. Information Collection and Use Word of the Day does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Local App Preferences: Your selected languages (up to 5), theme preference (Light, Dark, or System Default), and cached word data are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device. No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the app. 2. Network Requests and Data Processing Daily Word Fetching: The app connects to the internet solely to download public daily vocabulary data (words.json) hosted on GitHub. No personal data, device identifiers, or tracking cookies are sent during these requests. No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party tracking tools, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks. 3. App Permissions Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly to fetch the daily word list. Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly to deliver local daily word notifications on your device. 4. Children's Privacy Word of the Day is suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group. 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. 6.
